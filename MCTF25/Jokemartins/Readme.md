# Mārtiņa-CTF – Meme Challenge Write-up

**Category:** Misc / Fun  
**Team:** Dikti cool ctf komanda  

---

## Challenge Description

> The greatest quality of any self-respecting hacker is their sense of humour.  
> Create a meme related to **Mārtiņa-CTF**, send it to **Mārtiņš #71** or **Mārtiņš #404** on Discord (along with your team name) and wait while the Cringe Council makes a decision.  
>
> The Cringe Council working hours are from **08:00 to 22:00 (EET / UTC+2)**.

Goal: create a Mārtiņa-CTF-related meme, submit it correctly, and receive the flag from the Cringe Council.

---

## Background – `martins.soup`

Earlier in the CTF there was a challenge involving a file called **`martins.soup`**.  
Inside it was a **corrupted PNG**, and our team never managed to recover the flag.

As a running joke we started repeating a popular Latvian meme phrase:

> **“huļi bļoda netīra”**  
> (roughly: *“why is the bowl dirty?”*)

This became the core inspiration for our meme.

---

## Our Meme

We created a **two-panel “expectation vs reality” meme** based on the soup challenge.

### Preview

![Mārtiņa soup meme](./martins-soup-meme.png)

> _Update the filename above if needed (e.g. `./meme.png`)._

### Concept

1. **Top panel – Expectations**

   - Image: a nice, clean bowl of soup.
   - Caption:  
     `what we expected from martins.soup`

2. **Bottom panel – Reality**

   - Image: a heavily glitched / corrupted-looking image, representing the broken PNG.
   - Overlaid text:
     - `* detected: corrupted PNG`  
     - `* flag not found`  
     - `* huļi bloda netira`
   - Bottom line:  
     `Team Dikti cool ctf komanda`

The joke is that we expected a normal “soup” / easy file, and instead got a cursed PNG, no flag, and only our catchphrase: **huļi bļoda netīra**.

---

## Submission Process

1. Created the meme as a PNG image.
2. During Cringe Council active hours (**08:00–22:00 EET**) we sent the meme on Discord to:
   - `Mārtiņš #71` or `Mārtiņš #404`
3. Included our team name in the message:
   - **Dikti cool ctf komanda**

After review, the Cringe Council accepted the meme and replied with the flag.
